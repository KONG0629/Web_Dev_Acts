FAQS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Product Inquiries

Q: Are all Byte Beauty products cruelty-free?
A: Yes! All Byte Beauty products are thoughtfully made without harming nor testing on animals.

Q: Where are Byte Beauty products made?
A: Byte Beauty's products are made in the beauty capitals of the world: Korea, Taiwan, and Japan!

Q: Are all Byte Beauty products vegan?
A: Most Byte Beauty products are vegan. For more questions about our products, send us a message at shop@bytebeauty.com

Q; Is Byte Beauty FDA-certified?
A: Yes, All Byte Beauty products are FDA certified in the Philippines. You may verify this through the Philippine FDA website.

Q: Are Byte Beauty sun care products SPF tested?
A: Yes, All Byte Beauty SPF claims are true and backed by SPF testing conducted by our Korean and Taiwanese manufacturers.

Q: Are Byte Beauty products ingredients safe for sensitive skin?
A: Byte Beauty's products are formulated for all skin types and made with skin-caring ingredients to help protect and nourish the skin. If you have sensitive skin, we recommend conducting a patch test by applying a small amount of the product on your forearm.
If you do not experience any redness or itching, this indicates that the product will not cause you allergies. If you have allergies to certain ingredients, you may send us a message at shop@bytebeauty.com so that we can check the ingredients.

Q: Are Byte Beauty products pregnancy-safe?
A: Most Byte Beauty products are pregnancy-safe. Please check with your doctor for ingredients to avoid during your pregnancy. You may send us a message at shop@bytebeauty.com so that we can check the ingredients.

Q: How do I choose the right shade for my foundation or powder?
A: Our product listings contain visual shade guide matches for you to compare with your skin tone and undertone. Your skin tone is the main color of your skin, ranging from fair to deep skin. Your undertone is the natural shade within that color.

There are 3 common undertones: Warm, Neutral and Cool.

Q; For lip and cheek products, which shade should I choose?
A: All our shades have been formulated to match all skin tones. You may refer to our product listing shade guides for visual references.

Q: When does my makeup expire?
A: The best rule of thumb is to follow the period after opening which is usually 12 months for Byte Beauty products. Our manufacturing dates are placed on our packaging, with an average shelf life of 3 years.

For example, if the product was manufactured in January 2023, it must be opened on or before January 2025, so that it can be used within the recommended period after opening.

Q: What can you recommend for makeup beginners?
A: Byte Beauty has a wide assortment of multi-purpose, lightweight, and long-wearing makeup - perfect for beginners! Try the Second Skin Matte Serum Cushion Foundation SPFis for a light, on-the-go face base and the Lip Mallow Tints for a soft-matte pigment you can use on your lips, cheeks, and lids.

Q: What can you recommend for skincare beginners?
A: The most important skincare steps are cleansing, hydrating, and sun protection. Start with a gentle cleanser like our Hyaluronic Soothing Cleansing Gel, hydrate with our Hyaluronic + B3 Boost Water Cream, and protect your skin with our Hyaluronic Sun Serum SPFso+ PA++++.

Q: Can you notify me when an out of stock product is back in stock?
A: Please click the 'notify me' button on the corresponding product listing, and input your email address so we can send a notification once the product is back in stock.

Q: I can't find the product I am looking for, is it discontinued?
A: For more questions about our products, send us a message at shop@bytebeauty.com
